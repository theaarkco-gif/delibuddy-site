1. What Delibuddy is

Delibuddy is a free, community-based, not-for-profit app for food-delivery couriers, operated by MJH Sunny. It suggests where and when to wait for orders, based on what riders log and on public data, and it carries a community chat in which riders can talk to each other. It is independent of Uber Eats, Glovo, Bolt Food and every other platform, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

2. Using the app

  • You must be at least 18 and work as a courier on your own account.
  • You are responsible for your own safety. Never use the app while riding; every tap can wait for a stop.
  • Log only real information. Invented orders or waits harm every rider and may lead to your account being suspended.
  • Keep your login to yourself. One account per person.

3. No guarantees

Suggestions are predictions built from the data available. They can be wrong. Delibuddy does not guarantee any number of orders or any level of earnings, and is not responsible for decisions you make based on it, for platform policies, or for anything that happens on the road.

4. Your data and the community

What you log stays yours; you can export or delete it at any time. Anonymous aggregates (for example, the average wait at a corner) are shared with all riders and may remain after you delete your account. In the chat you appear under a handle, not your real name, except that the admin of a private group you join sees your real name and email address. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

5. The community chat

  • Treat other riders as you would at a restaurant door. Abuse, harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content and spam are not allowed, and neither is posting somebody else's personal details.
  • Post what is true. A false alert about a restaurant, a road or a rider wastes people's time and fuel.
  • What you write in a room is read by everybody in that room. Do not post anything there you would not want passed on.
  • The operator can hide or remove any message, mute an account, or suspend an account, and will do so for anything in this section. Messages reported by several riders may be hidden automatically until they are reviewed.
  • Chat content is for the riders using the app. Copying it out, republishing it or feeding it into other services is not allowed.

6. The help alert

  • The help alert does not call the emergency services. It sends a message to the people in your own private groups, and to nobody else. No operator is watching for it and nobody is on duty to answer it. If you or somebody else is in danger, call 112 yourself.
  • Use it only for a genuine emergency. Sending a false alert, or using it as a joke or a test, can lead to your account being suspended, because it teaches people to ignore the next one.
  • End your alert when you are safe, so that the people coming to you know.
  • Nobody is obliged to answer an alert or to come to you, and answering one is your own decision and at your own risk. Delibuddy does not guarantee that an alert reaches anybody, or that anybody responds.
  • You can send at most three alerts in any 24 hours.

7. Private groups and their admins

  • A private group exists only after the operator approves it. People join it with an invite code from its admin, who can remove or block a member and can remove messages in that group.
  • If you are a group admin you will see the real name and email address of every member of your group. Use them only to run the group. Do not share them, publish them or use them for anything else.
  • Share a group invite only with people you mean to admit. If you leave a group, your place as admin passes to the longest-standing remaining member.
  • A group admin is responsible for what their group is used for. A group used to harass, to organise something unlawful, or to break these terms may be closed.

8. Fair use of the community data

The shared statistics are for riders using the app. Scraping, reselling or feeding them into other services is not allowed.

9. Changes and ending the service

The app is run by volunteers and may change or stop at any time. Terms may be updated; the current version is always on this page.

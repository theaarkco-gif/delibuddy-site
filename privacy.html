Delibuddy — Privacy Policy (draft for app stores)

Who we are

Delibuddy is a community-based, not-for-profit project operated by MJH Sunny, a delivery rider in Lisbon, Portugal. It is run by riders, for riders. Contact: theaark.co@gmail.com.

What the app does

Delibuddy helps food-delivery couriers decide where and when to wait for orders. It never connects to, automates, or reads data from Uber Eats, Glovo, Bolt Food or any other platform. Everything it knows comes from what you type or tap, from files you choose to import, and from public data sources (weather, holidays, sports fixtures, OpenStreetMap).

Data we collect

  • Account: email, display name, password (stored hashed), language.
  • Profile: vehicle type, platforms you use, starting zone, working hours, daily target, radius, whether you ride in rain, your Uber minimum rate setting.
  • Activity you log: shift start/end, order taps (got, arrived, picked up, delivered, cancelled), the restaurant you pick, the fee you enter, tips, your Next-move choices and quick observations.
  • Location: a precise GPS point at each tap, and a point every 90 seconds while your wait timer is running and the app is open. We never track location in the background or while the app is closed.
  • Imports: orders you import from your Uber weekly statement CSV.
  • Technical: app version, device model, sync errors.

How we use it

  • To build your personal statistics (euros per hour by zone, corner, hour).
  • To compute community aggregates that all riders see: average wait per corner, expected orders per zone, number of riders currently in a zone.
  • To improve the prediction model (a nightly statistical model and, when enabled, an AI assistant that explains recommendations).

What other riders can see

Only aggregates: a corner's average wait, a zone's expected value, a count of riders in a zone. Never your name, your income, your trail, or that you were at a specific place at a specific time.

Where data is stored

On your phone (local database) and, when cloud sync is on, on Supabase servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU). Backups are kept for 7 days.

AI assistant

When the owner enables it, the app may send an anonymous summary of the current situation (zone, hour, weather, expected values, no personal identifiers) to Anthropic's Claude API to write an explanation in plain words. You can see whether it is enabled in Settings.

Your rights (GDPR)

Export all your data from Settings at any time. Delete your account from Settings: your identity and personal records are removed within 30 days; anonymous contributions to community averages (a wait time at a corner) may be kept. Write to theaark.co@gmail.com for access, correction or full erasure.

Retention

Personal activity data: 24 months, then anonymised. Account data: until you delete the account.

Children

Delibuddy is for adults who work as couriers. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We will post changes on this page and in the app.

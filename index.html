Where to wait, and when.

Delibuddy is a free, community-based, not-for-profit app for food-delivery couriers in the Lisbon metro area, built and operated by a rider (MJH Sunny), working with Uber Eats, Glovo or Bolt Food. Riders log their own waits and orders with one button; the app learns which corners get orders at which hours, and tells every rider where to wait next.

How it works

  • Log each order with four taps at natural stops: Got order, Arrived, Picked up, Delivered. Never while riding.
  • After each delivery, see your next move: stay, go nearby, or go back, ranked by expected earnings after travel time.
  • Your own euros per hour by zone, corner, hour and weekday, plus which kitchens make you wait.
  • Community data spreads riders out instead of stacking them in one place.

What it never does

It never connects to or automates the delivery platforms' apps. Everything it knows comes from what riders log and from public data such as weather and football fixtures.
